SPROCKET RALLY  -  Windows x64

Run:  sprocket.exe

Needs: 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 and a graphics driver with Direct3D 12 or Vulkan.
SDL3.dll is beside the game and must stay there.

Controls (keyboard / gamepad)
  Steer            arrows            left stick or D-pad
  Gas / brake      Z or Up / X or Down    A / X, or the triggers
  Hop, hold to drift   Shift or C    R1
  Use item         Space or V        L1 or B
  Pause            Esc               Start
  Menus            arrows, Enter, Esc     stick or D-pad, A, B
  4:3 / 16:9       Tab or F1         Back
  CRT shader       Q / E             L3 / R3

One to four players: GRAND PRIX and SINGLE RACE ask how many. Two split the screen, three
or four take a quarter each. Players one and two have keys (arrows / WASD); gamepads go to
the last players first, so players three and four need one each.

TIME TRIAL: alone with three nitros against the ghost of your best run on that track.

OPTIONS (main menu or pause menu): window, borderless or fullscreen (F11 switches),
which monitor, and CONTROLS to rebind every key and pad button.

Records are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\sprocket-rally\records.txt.
